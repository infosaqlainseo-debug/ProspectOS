# ProspectOS Agents Constitution

## PROJECT
ProspectOS

## PURPOSE
A production-grade AI Agentic SEO Prospect Intelligence Platform.

## PRIMARY FUNCTION
Discover companies, collect public company/website information, analyze websites, enrich records with authorized SEO/domain metrics, discover official social profiles, verify information, maintain historical records, and provide traceable intelligence.

## PHASE 1 SCOPE
- Company discovery
- Website discovery
- URL normalization
- Company deduplication
- Website crawling
- Website analysis
- Social profile discovery
- SEO/domain metric enrichment
- Data validation
- Evidence/provenance
- Risk indicators
- Website health indicators
- Historical records
- Persistent storage
- Job management

## PHASE 1 MUST NOT INCLUDE
- Automated cold email
- Spam
- Client outreach
- Backlink purchasing
- Marketplace matching
- Automated sales
- CRM automation
- Payment processing

Those belong to future phases.
